## Findings — the data/control/claim boundary

### 1. Reference material does not carry the same kind of truth

| Input the user might have | What is actually known from the *input form* | What the image editor receives; claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary site/building photo**, façade or interior | The camera captured an appearance at a time and viewpoint; perspective/occlusion can hide dimensions or structural state [N] (source: [P04](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-a-digital-orthophoto-quadrangle-doq-or-orthoimage), [S08](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11889042/)) {decay: 2027-09-29}. | Visible pixels, not unseen property lines, hidden structure, wind exposure, or measured levels [N] (P04,S08) {decay: 2027-09-29}. A generated façade proposal is not photographic evidence of the current building. |
| **Verified orthoimage** with its data/metadata | Unlike an ordinary aerial photo, geometric displacements from relief and tilt have been removed so it has more uniform scale [D] ([P04](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-a-digital-orthophoto-quadrangle-doq-or-orthoimage)) {decay: none}. | Once re-generated, the pixels are **not** automatically a georeferenced orthophoto, even if they look overhead [N] (P04,[S15](https://www.washington.edu/news/2021/04/21/a-growing-problem-of-deepfake-geography-how-ai-falsifies-satellite-images/)) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |
| **ArcGIS map/layout raster** (possibly aerial basemap plus vector overlays) | An ArcGIS Pro map export fixes extent and map scale from its view while pixel size is separately adjustable [D] ([P01](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/help/sharing/overview/export-a-map-or-layout.html)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. The underlying feature layers and credits are separate from the raster rendering [D] ([P15](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/access-use-constraints.htm)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | Pixel enlargement or AI-added buildings does not update feature-layer coordinates or validate a boundary [N] (P01,P04) {decay: 2027-09-29}. Item and basemap licences remain relevant (P14–P16). |
| **Google Maps/Earth image or place screenshot** | Google sets **different product- and medium-specific conditions** for Maps, Earth, Street View and Map Tiles API [D] ([P12](https://about.google/intl/en-GB_ALL/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/), [P13](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | An AI output's resemblance to a map gives neither geospatial accuracy nor reuse permission [N] (P04,P12,P13,S15) {decay: 2027-09-29}. Screenshot allowance is not a blanket licence for significant generative transformation. |
| **Revit rendered view / sheet image** | Revit distinguishes view-to-raster export from IFC/model-data export [D] ([P02](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Revit-DocumentPresent/files/GUID-A5E61A08-9635-44D0-93CA-75C15282119B.htm)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. Original parametric/BIM workflow research treats editable design data and AI visualization as different stages [R] ([P17](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-design-society/article/generative-aipowered-parametric-modeling-and-bim-for-architectural-design-and-visualization/A76987B083B87FA9E8579B1DCA532A0B)) {decay: none}. | A generated view may visually imply a wall, window or structural member that does not exist in the Revit model [N] (P02,P17,[S01](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-8945/5/4/94)) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |
| **AutoCAD drawing exported to JPEG** | JPGOUT exports selected objects as a JPEG, not as editable CAD objects [D] ([P03](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/ENU/AutoCAD-Core/files/GUID-1A2C1CB7-2323-456E-AF23-1FBA900E5EFC.htm)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | Line weight, crop, annotations and text may affect appearance; the generator cannot infer authoritative dimensions merely from that screenshot [N] (P03,P17) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |
| **Structure/massing/model viewport** | One IAAC project used a physical massing photo and CAD/depth-derived views for distinct façade and plan-like images [O] ([P19](https://blog.iaac.net/controlled-creativity/)) {decay: none}. | A depth map or silhouette constrains aspects of an output, not a material schedule, load path or coordinated plan [N] (P19,[P22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03068)) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |

[N] (P02,P03,P04,P17,[S02](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/16/14/2882),[S03](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S209526352400147X),[S04](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/13/2270)) {decay: 2027-09-29} **Four separate validity questions** recur: *Is the verbal brief well specified? Does this one picture retain the important visible features? Do multiple views depict one plausible design? Does an editable model meet actual dimensions, services and code requirements?* Evidence for one does not resolve the others. Architectural reviews distinguish image, plan, 3D form, façade and technical stages; the broad AECO review extends the distinction to construction/operations, but none certifies a specific generator output.

### 2. Provider controls: similar names, different semantics

| Provider/product | What its **own** documentation establishes | Limitation visible from the evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI image API** | Generates from text or edits an existing image with image+instruction; edit masks/endpoints and conversational revision vary by route [D] ([P05](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | Feature support is not a measured guarantee that unmasked site/building pixels remain identical [N] (P05,[S05](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.17525v1)) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |
| **Google Gemini image models** | Image+text editing and iterative revision are documented; the page warns users to have rights to uploaded images [D] ([P06](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | Gemini editing permission does **not** grant permission to upload third-party Google Maps/Earth imagery [N] (P06,P12) {decay: 2026-12-28}. |
| **Black Forest Labs FLUX.2** | Supports multiple input images with explicit role assignment and **does not support a negative-prompt parameter** in this guide [D] ([P07](https://docs.bfl.ai/guides/prompting_guide_flux2)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | References are interpreted, not geometrical coordinate locks; model and endpoint limits can change [N] (P07,[P22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03068)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. |
| **Midjourney Image Prompts** | Uses input images as **inspiration** for content/composition; docs route precise changes to the separate Editor; image weight changes influence [D] ([P08](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32040250122381-Image-Prompts)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | Image Prompt alone is not evidence of pixel/plan fidelity [N] (P08,P21) {decay: 2027-09-29}. |
| **Stability AI API** | Exposes distinct Edit routes (e.g., inpaint) and Control routes (e.g., sketch/structure/style), with endpoint-specific fields including `negative_prompt` where documented [D] ([P09](https://platform.stability.ai/docs/api-reference)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | A negative field in one API cannot be copied verbatim into FLUX.2; all controls remain conditional guidance [N] (P07,P09) {decay: 2026-12-28}. |
| **Photoshop Generative Fill** | Active selection plus optional reference image and model-dependent object/scene intent [D] ([P10](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/create-open-import-images/create-images/use-reference-images-for-consistent-results.html)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | User reports of changes beyond a selected region are version-specific `[O]`, not a universal failure frequency ([S13](https://community.adobe.com/questions-712/changes-to-generative-fill-1174820)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. |
| **Chaos Veras / Enscape** | Uses the live viewport as substrate, with Geometry Override, Material Override, seed, prompts, selected-area edits and design-style reference documented [D] ([P11](https://documentation.chaos.com/space/EREVIT/128418464/Veras+for+Enscape)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. | “Lower Geometry Override promotes detail retention” is the **vendor's control description**, not an independently tested preservation rate; an earlier hands-on article reported geometry drift [O] ([S09](https://aecmag.com/visualisation/veras-ai-based-renderer-for-revit-models/)) {decay: 2026-12-28}. |

[R] ([S05](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.17525v1),[S06](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13226v1)) {decay: none} Technical image-editing surveys distinguish *whole-image generation*, *local edit*, *preserving outside the target*, *structural conditions* and *evaluation metrics*; they do not validate architectural dimensional accuracy on their own. [O] ([P19](https://blog.iaac.net/controlled-creativity/),[S10](https://cgconnect.chaos.com/insights/articles/4c85abff-exclusive-cgdream-review)) {decay: 2027-09-29} An IAAC thesis workflow and a CGDream hands-on review illustrate that a submitted 3D model, a viewport render, a depth image, a segmentation mask, and a style photograph constrain *different* aspects of appearance. P19 used dated 2023 tools; S10 was invited by a vendor in 2024. Neither is a current cross-platform benchmark.

### 3. Direct research versus attractive-prompt inference

- [R] ([P20](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-8945/6/3/155)) {decay: none} A 2026 pilot used 55 **text-only** architectural prompts from 22 students at one institution and reported a strong relationship between its **own prompt-richness and output-richness scores**. The authors explicitly did **not** test transfer to image editing, measured design quality or BIM. “More adjectives preserve a measured plan” is **not** this result. 
- [R] ([P21](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/14/7/771)) {decay: none} Jung's 2026 study coded **151 prompt episodes in 20 student team projects**. In this dataset, reported *violated prompt–output alignment* was **8/62 (13%)** in a concept-led task and **27/89 (30%)** in a site-specific task. The authors distinguish discrete feature mistakes from harder-to-repair **circulation, adjacency and boundary** breakdowns. Episodes are nested in teams; tool type, year and task constraint intensity co-varied, so the comparison is **descriptive**, not a general model error rate or a causal estimate. Authors observed a human-defined spatial structure → bounded AI enrichment → critical review/repair workflow, especially in higher-constraint cases.
- [R] ([P22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03068)) {decay: none} Du et al.'s CAADRIA 2025 study treats **cross-view consistency** as a separate technical research problem and uses multiple shoebox-model views, style/structure/angle losses and depth-aware attention. It does **not** find that a shared seed or repeated text prompt alone makes views match. [O] ([P19](https://blog.iaac.net/controlled-creativity/)) {decay: none} The IAAC case similarly assigns different structural/style conditions to front, rear, interior and plan-like outputs, but supplies no population-wide error rate.
- [R] ([P17](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-design-society/article/generative-aipowered-parametric-modeling-and-bim-for-architectural-design-and-visualization/A76987B083B87FA9E8579B1DCA532A0B),[P18](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-025-01826-4)) {decay: none} Ko et al. demonstrate a hybrid parametric/BIM-plus-visualization workflow. A separate specialist heritage-façade study used **curated local data + LoRA + ControlNet + professional prompts**, not generic text alone; its trained arcade-façade setting does not establish arbitrary-building or conservation-grade fidelity. [R] ([S01](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-8945/5/4/94),[S02](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/16/14/2882),[S03](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S209526352400147X),[S04](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/13/2270)) {decay: none} Four separate architectural/AECO reviews contextualize these workflows, but shared cited literature is **not four replications** of the original findings.

### 4. Provenance and usage rights are source-specific

| Distinct permission question | Source-bounded statement |
|---|---|
| **Can a Google view be annotated, significantly transformed, or used commercially?** | [D] ([P12](https://about.google/intl/en-GB_ALL/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/)) {decay: 2026-12-28} Geo Guidelines allow some labeled overlays/screenshots in defined settings, restrict significant alteration of Earth/Earth Studio imagery absent clear simulation context, **and separately prohibit commercial/promotional use of Google Earth content**. Certain satellite-view promotional uses have Earth-like restrictions. Disclosure is necessary for a specified altered-Earth case but **not sufficient** to override the commercial prohibition. Actual use and product must be identified first. |
| **May an application feed map tiles to an image-analysis/model pipeline?** | [D] ([P13](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies)) {decay: 2026-12-28} The **Map Tiles API** policy permits visualization and expressly lists image analysis, machine interpretation and geodata extraction as disallowed non-visualization uses, subject to its agreement; attribution is required. This is a **Tiles API rule**, not proof that every Google mapping product has identical restrictions. |
| **Can an ArcGIS Online-hosted static map be shared or transformed?** | [D] ([P14](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/static-maps.htm),[P15](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/access-use-constraints.htm),[P16](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/display-copyrights.htm)) {decay: 2026-12-28} Esri lists limited static-map uses including specified internal/client reports/presentations and requires visible credit; other uses require permission. Items/layers can carry additional terms. Esri-hosted ArcGIS Online maps also disallow unattended robotic fetching. Static display permission does **not** itself establish permission for third-party AI ingestion or sale of derivatives. |
| **What if a photo, drawing, or AI output belongs to somebody else?** | [O] ([S12](https://www.ribaj.com/intelligence/copyright-in-the-age-of-ai/),[S18](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/4e9b05b9/infringement-risk-relating-to-creation-and-use-of-the-output-of-a-generative-ai-system)) {decay: 2027-09-29} UK architectural reporting raises client/author/photographer and upload-term issues; a multinational legal analysis highlights jurisdiction-dependent output similarity questions. Neither establishes Philippine rights or permission to disclose a particular client's BIM/site information. |

[D] ([P04](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-a-digital-orthophoto-quadrangle-doq-or-orthoimage)) {decay: none} The USGS distinguishes a correction-controlled orthoimage from an ordinary air photograph. [O] ([S15](https://www.washington.edu/news/2021/04/21/a-growing-problem-of-deepfake-geography-how-ai-falsifies-satellite-images/)) {decay: none} UW's **secondary university news account** describes plausible falsified satellite views reported by its researchers; the inaccessible full original paper was not treated as read. [N] (P04,P12,P14,S15) {decay: 2027-09-29} A stylized site composite and a reference map therefore have different evidentiary and licence states, even when rendered in one frame. [O] ([S08](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11889042/)) {decay: 2027-09-29} A geodesign perspective likewise places local datasets and stakeholder context beyond what a single map image visually communicates.

[D] ([P23](https://www.riba.org/work/insights-and-resources/ai-report/)) {decay: none} RIBA's **2026 survey landing page** says 74% of the practices it surveyed use AI in at least some projects; this is **not** a global or Filipino adoption rate, and its linked full report PDF was not examined. [O] ([S11](https://www.ribaj.com/intelligence/ai-augmented-architectural-practice-from-the-inside/)) {decay: 2027-09-29} A practitioner writing in RIBA Journal describes early visual palettes as internal *conversation starters*, not final imagery. [O] ([S16](https://archinect.com/forum/thread/150521508/i-feel-like-ai-use-is-unethical-for-architects)) {decay: 2027-09-29} A January 2026 Archinect thread contains both a material-vibe-check argument and concern that polished schematic visuals imply decisions on massing/setbacks/materials have already been solved; it is self-selected conversation, not a poll.

[O] ([S07](https://www.archdaily.com/1034327/the-plan-and-the-prompt-how-ai-is-rewiring-design-and-practice),[S17](https://www.archdaily.com/1036357/how-top-firms-see-ai-shaping-architectures-workflows)) {decay: 2027-09-29} An ArchDaily essay examines the risk that generated images over-promise joints, material thickness and constructability, while attributed interviews with several firms discuss the architect's need to curate and translate visual options into buildable proposals. AIRI's selected respondents are not a representative survey. [O] ([S09](https://aecmag.com/visualisation/veras-ai-based-renderer-for-revit-models/),[S10](https://cgconnect.chaos.com/insights/articles/4c85abff-exclusive-cgdream-review),[S13](https://community.adobe.com/questions-712/changes-to-generative-fill-1174820),[S14](https://community.graphisoft.com/t5/Visualization/Native-ArchiCAD-AI-Visualization-Plugin-Seeking-Feedback/td-p/690808)) {decay: 2026-12-28} AEC/CG hands-on reports, a Photoshop complaint thread, and a Graphisoft forum developer's account give **version-bound, non-comparable** observations on changed geometry/selection borders. The developer's “9 times out of 10” client-ready impression is self-promotional, not an independently audited success rate.

## Conflicts & uncertainties (preserved side by side)

1. **Prompt richness versus spatial trust.** P20 reports a strong association for **text-only** output richness in one student setting `[R]`; P21 reports site-specific relational failures in a *different* student setting `[R]`. Neither negates the other. Whether one added clause improves geometry preservation for this user's source files remains `[UNVERIFIED]` (P20,P21) {decay: 2027-09-29}.
2. **Vendor controls versus field observations.** P11 says low Geometry Override promotes retention `[D]`; S09 describes older-render drift `[O]`, and S14 offers a developer's optimistic 2026 account `[O]`. Versions, assessments and image tasks differ; no single current model-wide preservation figure follows (P11,S09,S14) {decay: 2026-12-28}.
3. **Masks versus immutable surroundings.** P10 documents selection and reference inputs `[D]`; S13 posters describe out-of-selection changes `[O]`. Whether current settings/model affect this user's unselected pixels is `[UNVERIFIED]` (P10,S13) {decay: 2026-12-28}.
4. **Altered-image disclosure versus map rights.** Google P12 requires clear simulation context for significantly altered Earth/Earth Studio imagery `[D]` **and** forbids commercial/promotional Earth-content use `[D]`. These are simultaneous terms, not opposing permissions; labeling cannot license a barred use (P12) {decay: 2026-12-28}.
5. **Coverage/rights gaps.** No actual source image, Google product/item licence, Esri layer licence, project contract, on-site survey, Philippine regulatory/heritage requirements, or model benchmark on a ground-truth site was supplied. They remain `[UNVERIFIED]` (S012 scope record) {decay: 2027-09-29}. A 2021 cartography original full text and 2026 RIBA PDF were not read; cannot inherit claims exclusive to them.
