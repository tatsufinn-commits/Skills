**Qualification method.** For each row, `G` is relevance to a declared gap (G1 source epistemology, G2 provider controls, G3 validation, G4 rights, G5 practitioner failures). `Authority` identifies first-party provider/author, original investigators, independent reviewer, or interested/anecdotal participant; **primary** means direct documentation or original research about the specific claim, **secondary** means synthesis/commentary/field testimony, not necessarily low quality. `Distinct use` says which report decision it changes or which independent cross-check it supplies; if absent, reject rather than count. `Claim / limit` is a bounded claim-fit check; grades follow `docs/EVIDENCE_TAXONOMY.md` and apply to claims, not entire sources. Source identity/URL, access, authority, context, claim fit have been checked on the readable page; no assertion of original media-file integrity or reproduction of external experiments. All linked sources were retrieved/read as HTML or, for P22, its arXiv PDF; date of an undated living documentation page = accessed date, not purported publication date. Volatile product controls and policy facts are recheck-due by 2026-12-28; stable-domain research interpretation by 2027-09-29; dated study findings as historical records have no decay, but **extrapolations do**. Source independence is **claim-specific**: two docs from the same company do not count as independent triangulation of performance; derivative news about a survey does not become a second survey.

## Deduplication, rejected candidates, and gap audit

- **Count:** 23 qualified primary (≥10) and 18 qualified secondary (≥18), **each attached to a distinct report role** above. Provider docs are primary for what they say about **their own features**; not independent tests of quality. S01–S06 share some general literature; they qualify only for their distinct task-specific framing, not six independent replications of the same accuracy claim. S11/S12 are different editorial pieces by different authors; neither independent of RIBA survey P23 for the survey number.
- **Excluded, not tallied:** Google/ArcGIS tiles/images themselves (no media/download rights verified), original Taylor & Francis 2021 deepfake-geography full article (get-access stub), RIBA 2026 full survey PDF (≈18.3 MB, skipped), any Reddit fetch returning 403, National Geoportal viewer error, outdated Adobe/Chaos/BFL/RIBAJ/AJ/USGS links that resolved 404, and derivative Beale Law summary of RIBA numbers (no independent survey); no paywall content pretended read. Geographic site documents, licensed client imagery, actual CAD/BIM model, provider accounts, and Philippine project contract/land/survey evidence were **not** supplied or accessed.
- **Independence/triangulation:** draw practical cautions from P21 (empirical studio), P22 (technical multi-view), P11 (vendor control existence) checked against S09/S13/S14 (version-bound field accounts). Rights must be checked against actual **rightsholder terms** P12–P16, with S12/S18 as interpretation only. Map integrity warning has P04 (USGS definition) + S15 (report of falsification); neither certifies any particular scene. No Core or long_term promotion sought; this is short_term research only.
- **Conflict register at claim level (not false equivalence):** FLUX.2 P07 explicitly lacks negative prompt field whereas Stability P09 and Veras P11 document negative-prompt controls: **different products, not contradiction**. P11 describes geometric retention settings while S09 reports drift in an earlier hands-on: feature availability ≠ successful output; not an empirical head-to-head. S14 developer's 'client ready' impression vs S09/S13 failures and P21 site-alignment breakdowns: distinct versions/tasks/assessors, cannot adjudicate a universal success rate. P20 text richness association vs P21 relational-logic failures: different modality/outcome, coexist. P12's simulation disclosure rule vs Earth commercial prohibition: both apply, the former never supersedes the latter.
- **Remaining gaps:** no user file to run paired input/output tests; no version-pinned architecture cross-provider benchmark with ground-truth dimensions, true multisensor coordinates or validated preservation guarantee; no exact Google/Esri item-specific rights for a concrete image; no review of local Philippine planning/fire/access/building codes or client NDAs. Maintain `[UNVERIFIED]` for any real site claim, construction compliance or specific output licence until verified by the responsible architect/rightsholder. **No quota shortfall.**
