## Short answer
**Yes.** The biggest likely missed step is **separating what the image is supposed to control**: your building's structure, the *look* of a precedent, and the *specific region* you want changed are different inputs. A stronger prompt cannot reliably make an anonymous, cropped screenshot into a measured building model. Structural guides and controlled edits can improve steerability **as documented**, but none guarantees exact dimensions, undisturbed pixels or matching new views. [E01–E04](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md), [E07](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt), [P21/P22](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]

Two other overlooked gates matter to a student: an internet screenshot is **not a reuse licence** [E13](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/news/ipophl-answers-faqs-on-photography-and-copyright/), [E15](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/29508?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) [N]; and a compelling image is **an illustration, not standalone proof of built dimensions, approvals or who originated the underlying design** [E08](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20275), [E23](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/explainer/Explainer.html), [P02/P21](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N].

## Pick the job before picking a tool

| What you actually need | Better input and control | Boundary to say out loud |
|---|---|---|
| **Ideas / atmosphere** while the design is open | Your sketch or massing plus a described palette or *cleared* style reference. A style reference guides appearance [E02](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-images/generate-images/reference-images-for-styling.html) [D]. | Interesting imagery is not a resolved plan or site study [E24](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/16/14/2878) [N]. |
| **Restyle an existing project** without redesigning it | Export a clean, same-camera view from *your* CAD/BIM/3D model; use source-image editing, or a composition/edge/depth guide where the chosen tool supports it [E01](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-images/generate-images/match-image-composition-to-reference-image.html), [E03/E04](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md), [P02](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. | Composition strength and ControlNet-style guidance steer generation; compare openings, grids and roofline to the source afterward [E09](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01571) [N]. |
| **Change one facade/sky/material area** | Use an actual area/selection editor, such as Midjourney Editor Paint/Restore or ChatGPT Images' selection editor; retain the original as a comparison [E05](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32764383466893-Editor), [E07](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt) [D]. | Mask edges and even areas outside a selection can change; if unmodified pixels are essential, independently inspect or composite them from the original [E07](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11084440-images-in-chatgpt), held [S13](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. |
| **Move/add actual doors, floors or bays** | Alter the source drawing/model **first**, then render/edit its new view. Structured facade-grammar research demonstrates why textual or masked changes alone are hard [E09](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01571) [N]. | AI changes in a PNG are not new Revit/AutoCAD objects [P02/P03](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. |
| **Show another side of a building** | If you own a 3D model, export that camera angle and edit each view against the *same* model. | A new angle prompted from one web photo invents hidden surfaces; Midjourney offers the creative operation, not geometric recovery [E06](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/48495453462797-Edit-Model), [P22](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. |
| **Rescue a tiny screenshot** | Seek an authorized higher-resolution source first. If merely improving presentation, compare any conservative upscale with the unaltered file [E10/E11](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N]. | Creative upscale can invent convincing window mullions, letters and building detail [E10](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.07037v1), [E11](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/utility/image-upscale) [N]. |

These are **routes, not a provider ranking**. Availability, model/version, settings, price and fidelity on your image were not tested. For example, Adobe Firefly distinguishes *Composition* from *Style* references [E01/E02](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [D]; ComfyUI ControlNet needs a matching guide/preprocessor [E04](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/controlnet/controlnet) [D]; Midjourney distinguishes whole-image Retexture from selected-region edits [E05](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32764383466893-Editor) [D]. Pick the **operation** your actual account offers before adapting a prompt.

## A seven-step, student-sized protocol

1. **Declare the design question.** Write one sentence: “I am exploring [massing / facade material / daylight mood / site scene] and must preserve [camera, three floors, five bays, entry, circulation].” Keep concept work as sketches or simple massing if polished imagery would settle choices you have not made. In one small self-selected student workshop, AI imagery carried unrequested lighting/tectonic detail and sometimes reduced perceived authorship; this is a warning from that setting, not a general causal claim [E24](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/16/14/2878) [N].
2. **Start with the best lawful base.** For your own creation, export the view from the original model/drawing (and note its camera, drawing revision and aspect ratio); keep a separate untouched copy. Prefer your clean own linework or model view to an app screenshot with browser UI, tiny text and trees crossing facade lines: facade-control research identifies these obstructions as a failure mode [E09](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01571); image-analysis help warns of resizing/metadata and small/spatial detail limits [E12](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8400551-chatgpt-image-inputs-faq) [N]. Keep *real* site constraints and authorship visible in the archival original.
3. **If the starting image came from the internet, trace the item first.** Record its *original* photographer/creator, item page, source URL, date, licence/permission and any conditions. Google's usage-rights filter is only a way to find licence information; confirm the host/licensor [E15](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/29508?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) [D]. “No watermark,” “Pinterest pin,” “for class,” and “credits to owner” are not blanket licences [E13](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/news/ipophl-answers-faqs-on-photography-and-copyright/), [E14](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/news/ipophl-releases-statutory-fair-use-guidelines-to-clarify-rules-on-copyright-exceptions/) [N]. If rights are unclear, **hold off uploading/reimagining that photo**: instead describe a general aesthetic in your own words, make your own reference, request permission, or substitute a verifiable licensed item (for example, check a particular CC item or Pexels image and its terms) [E16–E19](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N]. Philippine fair use is fact-specific; this is a prudent workflow gate, not a legal finding that all private study is forbidden. Map/Street View/Esri screenshots need their *separate* product/item terms [P12–P16](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [D].
4. **Assign roles to references.** `A = my structure/camera` (model export, sketch, section); `B = cleared appearance only` (materials/light/palette); `C = edit area` (mask/layer). If you must change the building itself, edit the underlying model/drawing rather than trusting B or C to maintain dimensions [E01–E05](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md), [P02](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. A simple hand-cleaned structural sketch may be clearer than automatic edge detection on a cluttered web photo; this is an untested suggestion for your files, not a measured advantage [E09](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01571) [N].
5. **Make a controlled first pass.** Keep the *same* base/camera, target area, aspect ratio, provider/model version, prompt and available settings while changing only one factor—for example, structure-guide strength or material wording. Save the original and each output separately; record a seed if the tool exposes one, but do **not** assume seeds reproduce results across model/service changes. Use region edits for local changes and whole-frame retexture only when broad restyling is intended [E05/E07/E08](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N].
6. **Reject beautiful failures.** Check side by side against your original: (a) intended change is present; (b) floor/window/bay count, doors, roof, circulation and camera relations remain correct where required; (c) unselected areas have not drifted; (d) people, context, symbols and shadows are plausible; (e) another view agrees with the *same original model* if claimed. Treat a wrong preserved opening as a **fail**, not as an aesthetic trade-off. These checks derive from three distinct edit metrics in ImgEdit, architecture-facade control failures and S012 site-workflow observations; none supplies a measured success percentage for your tool [E08](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20275), [E09](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01571), [P21](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N]. Validate dimensions/site conditions in the original CAD/BIM/GIS or other project records, **not the AI image** [P01–P04](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md) [N].
7. **Present provenance and level of certainty.** Keep a small version trail (below) and caption an unverified image, for example: **“AI-assisted concept visualization based on my [dated] model view; material/light reimagined; building dimensions and site context must be checked against the original drawings.”** If a third-party reference is actually used, record/credit it according to its specific licence [E16/E18](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N]. C2PA credentials can aid edit history but cannot prove truth or rights and may be absent [E23](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/explainer/Explainer.html) [D]. Before coursework submission, check *your actual course/assignment* policy; do not treat any handbook example as permission [E20](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N].

### Copyable edit brief — only for your own or cleared base

> **Purpose:** concept study of the material and evening light on *my* three-storey studio model. **A / structure:** the attached original east elevation/model render; maintain this camera, three floors, five facade bays, central entry and roofline. **B / appearance only:** [my own or explicitly licensed] warm brick/rainscreen reference; borrow palette and texture, not its building form. **C / region:** change facade material inside the marked wall surfaces; retain glazing and the unselected scene. **Output:** one clearly labeled conceptual image. If a constraint cannot be held, prefer flagging the change over disguising it. I will compare the result with the original model; do not invent dimensions or construction approvals.

This is **untested**, and the `[A]/[B]/[C]` notation is a human input plan, **not provider syntax**. Some providers have no dedicated negative-prompt field (held FLUX.2 P07); translate the *desired final state* and the supported reference/mask controls to the actual product [P07](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-source-register.md), [E01/E05/E07](../Brain/short_term/notes/2026-09-29_arch-image-student-gap-register.md) [N]. If the only available item is someone else's unlicensed screenshot, this example is **not** a permission to upload it.

### One-page iteration and source log

| Field | Fill for each source / generated attempt |
|---|---|
| **Design question + source truth** | My sketch/model/photo or third-party item page; author, URL, access date, licence/permission/terms; drawing revision or known camera/scale; retain original separately. |
| **Study setup** | Concept / local edit / redesign / new view; A structure, B style, C mask; provider/product, model/version, account visibility/privacy, settings, prompt, optional seed, timestamp. |
| **Required invariants** | Observable bay/floor/opening count, roof/circulation/site relationship and camera orientation; flag which are *not* known from a web snippet. |
| **Result and reject reason** | Intended change present? preserved items/pass/fail? extra changes outside C? cross-view check? labelled concept vs verified drawing? Keep rejected outputs as learning records, not validation data. |
| **Academic/public use** | Instructor/assignment rule checked; licence credit (CC best-practice title, author, source, licence where applicable), AI tool URL/access date/detailed prompt, student's own decisions and checks; distribution destination. |
