# Legal Postures — four surfaces, gates not advisories

**Sources:** blind-spots leg §3–§5 · vicinity-maps v2 (PD 1096, PH governance) · student workflow step 3 & 7 · prompt kit eligibility gate (Skills repo `docs/`). PH-context facts decay **2026-12-28** unless noted. This is a student workflow gate, not legal advice; Philippine fair use is fact-specific.

## G1 — Rights pre-flight (before any upload)

- Eligible base: **own** model export / drawing / photo · **licensed** item (confirmed on the host/licensor — a usage-rights filter only *finds* licence info) · **cleared** by the rights holder.
- **Not blanket licences** (IPOPHL, documented): "no watermark," "Pinterest pin," "for class," "credits to the owner."
- Map / Street View / Esri screenshots: their **separate product/item terms** apply.
- Rights unclear → **do not upload**. Alternatives: describe an aesthetic in your own words · make your own reference · request permission · substitute a verifiable licensed item.
- Record for any internet-sourced item: original creator, item page, URL, date, licence, conditions.

## G2 — CMI (copyright management information)

- Never remove or obscure watermarks, credits, attribution — including "incidentally" during enhancement, upscaling, or cropping.
- Refuse such requests explicitly; offer the lawful alternative (own/licensed base).

## G3 — PD 1096 vicinity plates (2004 Revised IRR, Rule III)

- Vicinity map: within **2.00 km radius** (commercial / industrial / institutional) or **0.5 km** (residential), any convenient scale, showing **prominent landmarks or major thoroughfares**.
- SDP: technical description, boundaries, orientation, lot relationship; **existing buildings within and adjoining the lot shall be hatched**, distances between proposed and existing buildings indicated. Three independent copies.
- Review/signature reserved to a **Registered and Licensed Architect** — AI cannot sign.
- **Operative consequence:** AI may compose *presentation*, never *geographic truth*. The radius, the landmarks, the distances and the hatching are yours — generate/overlay them from your own vector layers, then hand-verify. A corpus plate audit caught an 18.6% radius error on a demo plate whose own annotation claimed 2 km: measure, don't trust.

## G4 — Disclosure (school / competition / public)

- Caption on the **presentation sheet** (not buried in a log):
  > *"AI-assisted concept visualization based on my [dated] model view; material/light reimagined; building dimensions and site context must be checked against the original drawings."*
- Reproducible attribution when a third-party reference is used: source, **access date**, URL, prompt description.
- Label speculative outputs **AI concept visualization** — never survey, as-built elevation, historical restoration, manufacturing drawing, or approved material schedule.
- Check the *actual* course/assignment policy before submission; a handbook example is not permission.
- Credits for provider-generated text/labels: place attribution, measured labels and captions **outside** the generative pass (layout/GIS/presentation software); never rely on generated text for legal attribution or a scale bar.

## Style authority (no-FoP, RA 8293)

The Philippines has **no freedom of panorama** for architectural works. Authority rungs, highest first — all lawful:

1. Your own precedent studies and project language.
2. Generic movement / period / palette language ("brutalist massing language," "warm minimal palette").
3. Deceased masters — works in the public domain (counsel for anything near the line).

**Excluded:** "in the style of [named living architect]" — refuse or reformulate to rung 2.
